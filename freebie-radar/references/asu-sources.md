# ASU sources for Freebie Radar

Every page here was publicly reachable on 2026-10-24 unless marked. Pages change: if one moved, search the
college's site instead of giving up. For the hidden-opportunities sweep, read each college's site with a web
search limited to it, for example `site:publicservice.asu.edu internship stipend "any major"`.

## Known hidden opportunity (example and test case)

| Opportunity | Posted by | Page | Why it counts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| McLEAPS, full-time Maricopa County internship (fall and spring) | Watts College of Public Service and Community Solutions | https://publicservice.asu.edu/impact/mcleaps-internships | The page says "open to both undergraduate and graduate students from any major at ASU"; ASU pages list a stipend and tuition waiver, with different amounts on different pages, so link the official page |
